1. Introduction

What is Bioinformatics?

  • Bio + information

    Bio ➡️ Human samples (with certain phenotypes)

    Information ➡️ Data (Omics)

Next generation sequencing (NGS)

Omics data is generated by NGS

What is difference between Sanger Sequencing and Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS)?

  • Sanger sequencing … ddNTP with flourchromes

  • NGS: library preparation + sequencing by synthesis

  • Library prep & bridge amplification

  • sequencing by synthesis (SBS)

  • NGS library molecule: adapter + insert

    • P5/P7 (binding to flow cell)
    • Index (Sample identifier, 6-12nt, multiplexing)
    • Primer (e.g., PolyA for mRNA, 6-mer for total RNAs)

Single-end vs. Paired-end Sequencing

3’ kit vs. 5’ kit (for mRNA capture)

Why 3’ is commonly used:

  • scRNA-seq standard: 10x Genomics Chromium uses the 3’ kit as the standard
  • Cost-effective and high throughput
  • Sufficient for quantifying gene expression levels
  • High mRNA capture efficiency due to poly-A tail targeting

When to use 5’ kits:

  • TCR/BCR V(D)J profiling: The CDR3 region is located at the 5’ end
  • Essential for studying T cell and B cell repertoires
  • Commonly used as a combination of the 10x Genomics 5’ kit and V(D)J enrichment

Omics data

  • holistic & comprehensive
  • high-dimensional … large p, small n
  • Multi-layered hierarchical … central dogma

FASTQ Format

  • information of sequence
  • four lines per read (n=20~30M)
@SRR21484222.626.1 626 length=51       ← Read ID
GCCTTGGTGGTGAAATGGTAGACTGGAATTCTCGGGTGCCAAGGAACTCCA  ← Sequence
+SRR21484222.626.1 626 length=51       ← Description
F:FFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFF:FFFFFFFFFFF:,FFFFFFFFF  ← Base call quality (Phred score)

Phred Quality Score

\(Q = -10 \log_{10} P\)

Phred Score Error rate Base call accruary
10 1 in 10 90%
20 1 in 100 99%
30 1 in 1,000 99.9%
40 1 in 10,000 99.99%

Central Dogma

We will focus on transcriptomes (RNA-seq data)

Why Bioinformatics?

✅ A single NGS experiment ➡️ GB to TB of information …far beyond what traditional lab methods can handle

✅ Rapidly declining NGS costs ➡️ exponentially growing data volume

General workflow of clinical researchers (?)

Excel에서 데이터 정리 → SPSS에서 분석 → 결과를 Word에 복붙 → 리뷰어 코멘트 → 처음부터 다시...
Warningdrawbacks of this approach
  • Data updated → Redo every table and figure manually
  • “Where did this p-value come from?” → Hard to trace back
  • Sharing your analysis with collaborators → Verbal explanation or screenshots

✅ Free & Open-Source >> SPSS
